# compact delegation hierarchy

## goal

make parent child-disclosure controls visually compact and keep indented child cards inside session-list and sidebar boundaries.

## success criteria

1. the visible disclosure label is compact while its accessible label retains the full child-agent action.
2. chevrons and `aria-expanded` clearly communicate collapsed and expanded state.
3. child cards retain indentation and a hierarchy connector.
4. child card width, padding, controls, and text fit inside mobile and desktop list boundaries.
5. child typography is denser than parent typography.
6. browser measurement coverage verifies containment, indentation, density, and disclosure state.
